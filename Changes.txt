            int8[N][M]*[K] is array of K elements of type pointer to an array of M elements of arrays of N elements of int8s

            'T[N] x' supports: 
                x[n] - T
                &x[n] - T*
                &x - T[N]*

            T* can be assigned T[] or T[N]* without explicit conversion
            Pointer arithmetic allowed only on T*

            @cast(T, x)

            Implicit cast rules:
                smaller integer type assigned to larger one, when signedness matches
                T[N]* assigned to T[]
